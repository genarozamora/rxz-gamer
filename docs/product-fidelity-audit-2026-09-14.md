channel. The published Kailh text conflicted with HUANO text printed on old

both brands, wireless4K guarantee, unconfirmed capacity and runtime, and other
revision-dependent figures. The current official page mixes older PAW3395
specifications and newer PAW3950MAX marketing; it is not a lot certificate:
https://attackshark.com/products/attack-shark-x3pro-8k-mouse
